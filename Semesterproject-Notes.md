# Block: Terrain & Water

**Example research questions:**

- How does DEM resolution affect watershed delineation accuracy in the pre-Alps?
- Which areas in municipality X are at highest risk of surface runoff under heavy rainfall?
- How do terrain derivatives (slope, aspect, TWI) differ between glaciated and non-glaciated catchments?
- Where are the hydrological bottlenecks in a given drainage network?

**Typical methods:** DEM processing, terrain derivatives (slope/aspect/hillshade), D8 flow direction, watershed delineation

# Block: Spatial Ecology & Occurrence

**Example research questions:**

- How has the reported range of species X shifted in Switzerland over the past 20 years?
- Does road density predict absences in occurrence records for species Y?
- How does sampling effort bias GBIF data in different land-use types?
- Which environmental variables best explain the distribution of an invasive species?

**Typical methods:** Point pattern analysis, KDE, IDW, occurrence data from GBIF/iNaturalist, sampling bias correction

# Block: Paths & Flows

**Example research questions:**

- How accessible are green spaces in Zurich by foot within 15 minutes?
- How does pedestrian network connectivity differ between rural and urban municipalities?
- What is the impact of a bridge closure on travel times in a small-town network?
- Which neighbourhoods have the worst accessibility to public transport stops?

**Typical methods:** Shortest path analysis, isochrone mapping, centrality measures, sfnetworks/R
